"Touch typing", also called "keyboarding", is a computer skill where you use all ten fingers to type without having to look down to find the right keys.

Touch typing skills help you write with ease and without effort. This will save you time and working at the computer becomes easier and much more enjoyable.

Emailing, instant messaging, writing up school reports - just about anything that you do with the computer, becomes a breeze. The more you practice the better you get - let's get started now!
