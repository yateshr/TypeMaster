Next, you will learn the letters E and I. You'll find them on the row above the home keys. E and I are typed using the middle fingers.

When reaching for E or I, or any key away from the homerow, leave all other fingers on their home keys. This is called anchoring. It makes it easy to return to the homerow position and keep your hands still.

Moving a finger away from the homerow may slightly lift the finger next to it--but keep that finger curved. When the "typing finger" returns to the homerow, the other one will mimic and fall back down on the home key.
