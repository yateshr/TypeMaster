WordTris is a typing game where you type words with the letters learned.

In the game, you will see a word in a moving beam. Your goal is to type the word without mistakes before the beam hits the bottom.

Instructions
1. To drop the beam, type the word in it and press Space.
2. The game is over once the box is full of missed beams or time is out.
3. Tip: It is easier to type the word if you look at the word display below the box and not the moving beam.
