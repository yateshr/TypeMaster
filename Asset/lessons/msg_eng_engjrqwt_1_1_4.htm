Try Not to Look Down
You will learn the key locations faster if don't peek at the keyboard. If you can't remember where a key is, look at the keyboard picture on screen to find it.

Fingers Curved, Wrists Flat
Keep your fingers lightly curved and wrists flat and straight when typing. This makes it easier to reach the keys and tap them lightly.

Say the Letters
Saying the letters out loud or silently in your head can help you learn and type at an even pace.
